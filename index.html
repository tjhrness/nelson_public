How to prepare your data
  1. In your spreadsheet, put each measurement series in a separate column. Name it IMR_Field or use a name such as IMR_Field_WaitTime. The prefix is case-sensitive.
  2. Keep rows in time order. Every selected cell must contain a finite number; do not include blanks, totals or units. Other columns, such as dates or notes, are ignored.
  3. Save as CSV UTF-8 (comma-delimited), then choose the file above. No Excel installation is needed to use the tool.

Moving ranges measure the absolute change from one row to the next, so the MR chart begins at point 2. Charts scroll horizontally when they are wide.

The eight pattern rules
  1. One point beyond 3 SD.
  2. Nine or more points on one side of the mean.
  3. Six or more points steadily rising or falling.
  4. Fourteen or more points alternating up and down.
  5. Two of three points beyond 2 SD, on the same side.
  6. Four of five points beyond 1 SD, on the same side.
  7. Fifteen or more points within 1 SD.
  8. Eight or more points outside 1 SD. Either side is allowed, including all on one side.

Points on a zone boundary are excluded using a relative tolerance of 10⁻⁹. Equal or near-equal consecutive values break trends. Overlapping qualifying windows are merged; only their qualifying points are marked. All rules are evaluated independently.
